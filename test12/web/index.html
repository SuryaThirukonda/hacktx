What happened
    Run summaryscored against the simulator's ground truth
    Everything on this page is simulated: the map, the people, the aircraft and the camera. People are found by the pretrained COCO YOLO26n model plus a shirt-colour check; the police car by its red and blue light bar (classical colour detection). The drone's positions are dead reckoning from the drone pad, never GPS; the police car pings its own position every second and the callouts say where the suspect is from it. Callouts are spoken with ElevenLabs (the browser's own voice if that is not available). The third-person and bird's-eye views are simulator cameras for watching; the drone only uses its own.
