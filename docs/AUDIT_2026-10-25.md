## 3. Known gaps and defects (carried over, still open)

1. **Skydio / MuJoCo removal, phase B** (memory note `dji-only`): `backend/third_party/skydio_x2/`,
   `sim/mujoco_drone.py`, scenarios OW7, PJ1, PJ2, `dynamics="mujoco"` / `stack="jev-x2"` fields, the
   `/api/sim3d/asset/x2.obj` route and README lines 55–61, 126–132, 204 are all still there. The decision was to
   delete, not swap.
2. **Orphans**: `missions/rescue.py` and `sim/survey.py` are in the tree but nothing imports them. Either wire them
   (rescue as new FR3/FF3 scenarios, survey into the map) or delete them.
3. **test12 loss rate**: 6 of 12 headless runs ended lost; 4 of 12 followed under 50 % of the time (basic_doc3 §6).
4. **3D socket traffic**: `/ws/sim3d` sends the survey grid in every 20 Hz frame (≈360 kB/s per socket on a 200 m
   world; worse on flood and SAR). Over a Cloudflare tunnel this is MB/s. Send the grid only when it changes.
5. **Access control**: with `OVERWATCH_TOKEN` set, 6 of 10 WebSockets still skip the check; the token travels as
   `?token=` in URLs (logs). Matters as soon as the tunnel is up for a demo.
6. **Stale docs**: `DEMO_RUNBOOK.md`, `README.md` (Skydio, old hero image), `test12/README.md` (removed views),
   `progress_2p.md`, `basic_doc1.md` (wrong remote), `IMPLEMENTATION_STATUS.md` (no entry for the squash or the
   live-bridge commit), `LIMITATIONS.md` (still says 22 world scenarios).
7. **Responder phone during a pursuit**: `POSITION_CALLOUT` events go to the dashboard and "phone speech" with
   relative kinematics (clock, range), and the dispatch's `POSITION_REPORT` (distance and bearing from the pad,
   lat/lon) goes to the dashboard event feed and the Tiger log, but **the responder PWA does not receive the
   pursuit position reports**: it gets briefs and alerts only. test12 has car-relative callouts, but only on its own
   page. This is the callouts to-do (section 4).
8. **Muse**: nothing in the tree. The merge plan's decision 10 was "No Muse" for pursuit; if Muse is for the Meta
   track it is a separate, additive piece (section 4).
9. Smaller: `/drive` only drives the main engine's people (not test12's suspect); reloading the City page stops a
   test12 run; spawn picking applies on Restart only; the DJI quad in 3D is drawn at 2–5× scale without a label.
